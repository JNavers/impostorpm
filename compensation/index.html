Glassdoor, LinkedIn, that one Slack thread. All wrong.

Salary data for Product Managers is broken in three ways. The Compass fixes each one.

1

Aggregated to mush

Median "PM salary" lumps a 1-year associate in Lisbon with a 12-year Director in Berlin. Useless for you.

2

Pulled from listings, not paychecks

Job-board ranges are negotiation anchors employers set. Real take-home pay looks different.

3

No bonus, no equity, no truth

Base is half the story. The Compass collects total comp: base, bonus, equity, and perks.

How we built the numbers.

Plain methodology, plain English.

Anonymous by design

No name, no employer, no email tied to your row. You stay invisible; the dataset stays clean.

PM-only sample

We screen role titles. Engineering managers and chiefs of staff don't dilute the dataset.

Open percentiles

Medians and quartiles, not vendor "estimates." Below 30 data points in a cohort, we say so on the chart.

Sample composition

Currently strongest in Iberia and Western Europe. Growing. We publish the composition with every result.
