Land offers a coworking space, workshops, gallery and periodic cultural events.

Land was born in the heart of Porto as a welcoming space for freelancers, companies, and other projects, with the mission of improving the working conditions of coworkers, in a relaxed but professional environment, creating dynamics between the most diverse business areas.

At the same time, Land also functions as a space for hosting events on a wide range of topics, from workshops to conferences, with diversified training being one of the strengths of this project.

Located in the Arts neighbourhood, Land also has a gallery space dedicated to exhibitions and other periodical cultural events.
