Next Level Hub offers mental health, well-being, and leadership programs to elevate you to the next level of development.

Next Level Hub is composed of nine expert psychologists who use an innovative approach based on behavioral sciences to shape people, teams, and organizations' mindsets, behavior, or performance. They promote sessions and training aimed at enhancing soft skills, fostering a positive organizational culture, and promoting mental health.

This partnership includes access to 1-on-1 personal sessions to explore topics ranging from career strategy and productivity to stress management and mental well-being (including support for challenges such as anxiety, burnout, depression). These personalized sessions can be held online or in person, at the Next Level Hub office in Porto.
